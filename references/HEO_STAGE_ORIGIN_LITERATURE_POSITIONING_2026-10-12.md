# HEO Stage-Origin Literature Positioning — 2026-10-12

## Purpose

This file defines what prior literature already establishes, what the present manuscript adds, and which claims require careful wording.

Use it during Introduction/Discussion review and final reference audit.

## Literature-established background

### Multistep spinel HEO conversion

Prior operando/ex situ work supports broad progression involving:

- progressive cation reduction;
- destabilization/reconstruction of the parent oxide;
- formation of reduced/metallic products with Li2O;
- substantial structural evolution across a broad lithiation interval.

Working key references in the current manuscript include:

- Huang et al., Chemical Engineering Journal 2021, 420, 129838.
- Luo et al., Advanced Science 2022, 9, 2201219.
- Wang et al., Nature Communications 2023, 14, 1487.
- Jin et al., Materials Today Chemistry 2025, 48, 102949.
- Li et al., Angewandte Chemie International Edition 2025, 64, e202518569.

Final publisher-level metadata verification is still required before submission.

### Ball-milling / microstructural effects

Prior work supports that milling/refinement/disorder/interface changes can alter HEO electrochemical utilization.

Therefore the manuscript must **not** claim that ball milling affecting capacity is new.

The present novelty is the stage-resolved localization of where the capacity gain primarily arises.

### Mg-containing HEO behavior

Prior work supports that Mg-containing high-entropy oxides can show altered conversion accessibility/stability and persistence of oxide-derived structures.

Therefore the manuscript must **not** claim that Mg lowering conversion capacity or stabilizing oxide-like environments is newly discovered here.

The present novelty is the stage-resolved localization of where the capacity loss is concentrated.

Exact close substitution/composition literature should be re-verified before final wording.

### GITT limitations / relaxation

Prior literature establishes that conventional GITT-derived apparent diffusion coefficients can become difficult to interpret under phase transformation, nonideal transport, finite geometry, and other departures from the standard assumptions.

Working references:

- Kang & Chueh, Journal of The Electrochemical Society 2021, 168, 120504.
- Jia et al., Journal of Power Sources 2022, 543, 231843.
- Zhu & Wang, Journal of Physical Chemistry C 2010, 114, 2830–2841.
- Horner et al., ACS Applied Energy Materials 2021, 4, 11460–11469.
- Deiss, Electrochimica Acta 2005, 50, 2927–2932.

State-dependent voltage relaxation itself is also not conceptually new.

Therefore avoid “first use of relaxation” claims unless a dedicated exhaustive novelty search proves them.

## Current novelty boundary

Strongest defensible novelty:

> **GITT relaxation is used as a stage-resolved electrochemical readout to identify which part of a known multistep conversion sequence is primarily responsible for the macroscopic performance change caused by material processing or composition.**

HEO-specific result:

> **Ball-milling capacity gain is dominated by expansion of the conversion-onset/progression region, whereas Mg-induced capacity loss is dominated by suppression of the subsequent late-conversion region.**

This is stronger and safer than claiming discovery of:
- multistep HEO conversion;
- ball-milling enhancement;
- Mg stabilization;
- GITT relaxation itself.

## Physical-assignment boundary

The paper may say that R2 is **associated with** oxide destabilization and structural reconstruction and R3 with continued evolution toward reduced metal/Li2O-derived states.

The paper should not say that GITT directly detects:
- M–O bond breaking;
- one specific metallic product;
- one cation-specific reduction sequence;
- one unique reconstructed phase.

The physical assignment is a synthesis of:
1. electrochemically defined relaxation/voltage regions;
2. relaxed-voltage behavior;
3. established conversion literature.

## Abstract literature strategy

The Abstract should not become a literature review.

Sentence 1 can state the accepted multistep conversion picture without citations in the Abstract if journal style permits.

The BM/Mg sentence should present the current experimental performance contrast and the new stage origin, not spend words proving that BM/Mg effects were already reported.

Detailed prior-art positioning belongs in the Introduction.

## Introduction literature logic

Recommended sequence:

1. establish known multistep HEO conversion chemistry;
2. identify the materials-development gap: overall capacity change does not reveal stage origin;
3. introduce GITT relaxation as state-resolved electrochemical information;
4. explain why BM and Mg provide useful opposite perturbations;
5. state the present stage-origin contribution.

Avoid a long history of GITT diffusivity criticism that makes the paper look method-centric.

## Claims currently allowed

- “multistep conversion” — allowed with literature support.
- “four electrochemically distinct regimes” — allowed as present analysis.
- “R2 associated with oxide destabilization/structural reconstruction” — allowed as broad assignment.
- “R3 associated with late conversion toward reduced metal/Li2O-derived states” — allowed as broad assignment.
- “BM capacity gain originates predominantly from R2 expansion” — present result.
- “Mg capacity loss originates predominantly from R3 suppression” — present result.
- “GITT localizes the reaction-stage origin of a materials performance change” — present contribution.

## Claims to avoid unless new evidence appears

- “first demonstration of GITT relaxation fingerprinting”
- “four new microscopic conversion steps discovered”
- “GITT directly detects M–O bond breaking”
- “BM universally accelerates conversion”
- “Mg slows conversion kinetics”
- “BM and Mg act independently/orthogonally”
- unique Mg-site or bond-level mechanism
- universal design rule that R2 must always be promoted or R3 always suppressed
