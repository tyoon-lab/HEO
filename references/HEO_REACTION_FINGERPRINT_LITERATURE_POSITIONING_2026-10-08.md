# HEO Reaction-Fingerprint Literature Positioning — 2026-10-08

## Purpose

This note records the literature gate used before rewriting the manuscript around the GITT reaction-fingerprint story.

## What is already established

### HEO conversion chemistry

Prior operando/ex situ studies already show that spinel HEO lithiation is multistage and reconstructive, with progressive multication reduction, oxide reconstruction, intermediate reduced oxides, metallic products, and Li2O-like conversion products.

Key examples already carried in the manuscript reference set:

- Huang et al., Chemical Engineering Journal 2021, 420, 129838.
- Luo et al., Advanced Science 2022, 9, 2201219, DOI 10.1002/advs.202201219.
- Jin et al., Materials Today Chemistry 2025, 48, 102949.
- Li et al., Angewandte Chemie International Edition 2025, 64, e202518569.

Consequence for claims:

Do not claim discovery of previously unknown conversion stages. Use the literature to support broad physical assignment of fingerprint-defined electrochemical regions.

### Ball milling / particle refinement

Ball milling, particle refinement, and crystallinity control have already been used to improve or alter HEO anode performance.

Example:

Chen et al., RSC Advances 2020, 10, 9736–9744, DOI 10.1039/D0RA00255K, used high-energy ball milling in a spinel HEO and attributed favorable storage to submicrometer particles, enlarged surface sites, and accommodation of volume change.

Consequence for claims:

Do not claim that ball milling improves HEO capacity/performance for the first time.

Remaining gap addressed here:

- where along the conversion trajectory reaction extent is redistributed after milling;
- whether the same modification changes relaxation persistence in the same or opposite direction.

### Mg / inactive-cation stabilization

HEO battery literature already discusses Mg as electrochemically less active and/or structurally stabilizing in conversion-type oxide systems.

Key examples:

- Sarkar et al., Nature Communications 2018, 9, 3400.
- Qiu et al., Journal of Alloys and Compounds 2019, 777, 767–774.
- Wang et al., Nature Communications 2023, 14, 1487.

Consequence for claims:

Do not claim discovery of Mg stabilization itself.

Remaining gap addressed here:

Mg-induced capacity loss is localized mainly to the late conversion-associated R3 regime while the t63 of the remaining R3 response changes only weakly.

### GITT limitations

Conventional GITT apparent-diffusivity interpretation is already known to be sensitive to reaction overpotential, phase transformation, geometry, finite-size effects, early transients, and interparticle heterogeneity.

Key examples:

- Kang and Chueh, Journal of The Electrochemical Society 2021, 168, 120504, DOI 10.1149/1945-7111/ac3940.
- Jia et al., Journal of Power Sources 2022, 543, 231843.
- Zhu and Wang, Journal of Physical Chemistry C 2010, 114, 2830–2841.
- Horner et al., ACS Applied Energy Materials 2021, 4, 11460–11469.
- Deiss, Electrochimica Acta 2005, 50, 2927–2932.

Consequence for claims:

Do not present direct relaxation analysis as if GITT-diffusivity limitations were newly discovered here.

### State-dependent relaxation

State-dependent voltage-relaxation time constants have already been examined in insertion-type lithium-ion systems.

Most directly relevant recent example:

Jorkesh, Akbari, Ahmed, and Habibi, Journal of Power Sources 2026, 682, 240338, DOI 10.1016/j.jpowsour.2026.240338. The work maps fast/slow time constants across SOC in NMC811 cells for OCV/SOC characterization.

Consequence for claims:

Do not claim first use of state-dependent relaxation time as a fingerprint.

## Remaining novelty

The present manuscript combines:

1. state-resolved GITT current-off relaxation;
2. fingerprint-derived electrochemical reaction regions;
3. relaxed-voltage / Q–OCV anchoring;
4. established HEO conversion chemistry for broad physical assignment;
5. a 2 × 2 BM/Mg materials perturbation design.

The resulting novelty is:

> **A relaxation-based reaction-state map identifies where different material modifications perturb conversion lithiation, separating redistribution of accessible reaction extent from persistence of the nonequilibrium relaxation.**

Material-specific result:

- BM: broader redistribution of accessible reaction extent (R1/R2/R4 up, R3 down) plus more persistent R2–R4 relaxation.
- Mg: selective loss of late conversion-associated R3 accessibility with only weak change in the remaining R3 t63.

## Manuscript-positioning rule

The paper should be written as a materials/conversion-mechanism paper using GITT as the diagnostic layer.

Avoid turning it into:

- a generic GITT-method paper;
- a four-stage mechanism-discovery paper;
- a Dapp-criticism paper;
- a ball-milling-performance paper;
- an Mg-stabilization paper.
