## Proposed abstract (1 words)

Changes in the total lithiation capacity of conversion-type anodes do not reveal where along overlapping reaction processes the additional or lost charge arises. This question is examined in spinel high-entropy oxide (HEO) anodes using ball milling and Mg incorporation as processing and compositional modifications. First-lithiation galvanostatic intermittent titration (GITT) is analyzed using the voltage-relaxation amplitude and characteristic relaxation time, with the rest-end quasi-open-circuit voltage providing supporting information. The evolution of these responses distinguishes four electrochemical regions and permits their nominal GITT-charge contributions to be compared across matched material pairs. Ball milling increases first-lithiation capacity in both composition pairs, with its largest positive regional charge increments in R2, associated with conversion onset and progression (+133 and +167 mAh g⁻¹), while charge in R3 decreases (−167 and −150 mAh g⁻¹). Mg incorporation decreases capacity in both processing pairs, with the largest negative regional changes concentrated in R3, associated with later conversion (−267 and −283 mAh g⁻¹). These results show that changes in overall capacity arise from unequal and sometimes opposing contributions across lithiation. Locating these contributions reveals which parts of the conversion response are most sensitive to processing and composition.

## Scientific anchoring
- Research problem: net lithiation capacity does not locate where processing/composition changes alter the response; reaction processes overlap.
- Matched samples: HEO, BM-HEO, Mg-HEO, BM-Mg-HEO. GITT relaxation amplitude and operational t63 are main electrochemical descriptors; finite-rest QOCV is supporting context.
- R1–R4 are **electrochemical regions** from L1/L3/L4, not unique isolated chemical steps.
- BM GITT R2: +166.7 (HEO→BM) and +133.3 (Mg→BM-Mg) mAh/g; R3 −150.0 and −166.7 respectively. Text presents ranges as rounded pair values and does not equate with GCD.
- Mg GITT R3: −266.7 (HEO→Mg) and −283.3 (BM→BM-Mg) mAh/g; earlier-region losses much smaller.
- First-lithiation GCD 0.1 C directional effects: BM positive and Mg negative in both matched pairs. GITT quantities are nominal pulse-charge, **not interchangeable** with GCD.
- No claim of experimentally proven Mg-rich residual oxide, uniquely identified phase per region, faster/slower intrinsic diffusion, or XPS-evidenced R3 chemistry.
