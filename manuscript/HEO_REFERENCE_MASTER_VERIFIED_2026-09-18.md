## A. Core reference chain used in the manuscript

### [1] Origin of entropy-stabilized oxides

**Rost, C. M.; Sachet, E.; Borman, T.; Moballegh, A.; Dickey, E. C.; Hou, D.; Jones, J. L.; Curtarolo, S.; Maria, J.-P.**  
*Entropy-stabilized oxides.*  
**Nature Communications** 2015, 6, 8485.  
DOI: **10.1038/ncomms9485**

**Directly supports:** configurational disorder/entropy as a route to stabilizing multication single-phase oxides.

**Chemical Engineering Journal** 2021, 420, 129838.

### [7] Direct spinel → mixed spinel/rock-salt → rock-salt pathway

**Jin, G.; Luo, C.; Wang, Z.; Jia, S.; Yu, H.; Zhang, C.; Wang, Q.; Zhang, B.; Wang, Z.**

**Role in present manuscript:** strongest primary source for assigning the late-stage GITT polarization hump primarily to phase transformation/conversion.

## B. Mg mechanistic references

### [8] Inactive MgO as stabilizing/spectator component

**Qiu, N.; Chen, H.; Yang, Z.; Sun, S.; Wang, Y.; Cui, Y.**  
*A high entropy oxide (Mg0.2Co0.2Ni0.2Cu0.2Zn0.2O) with superior lithium storage performance.*  
**Journal of Alloys and Compounds** 2019, 777, 767–774.  
DOI: **10.1016/j.jallcom.2018.11.049**

**Directly supports:** electrochemically inactive MgO formed during conversion can contribute to structural/cycling stability.  
**Boundary:** rock-salt HEO, not the present spinel; use as mechanistic precedent, not direct proof of Mg site/function in the current sample.

### [9] Mg-content dependence observed by operando TXM

**Wang, S.-Y.; Chen, T.-Y.; Kuo, C.-H.; Lin, C.-C.; Huang, S.-C.; Lin, M.-H.; Wang, C.-C.; Chen, H.-Y.**  
*Operando synchrotron transmission X-ray microscopy study on (Mg, Co, Ni, Cu, Zn)O high-entropy oxide anodes for lithium-ion batteries.*  
**Materials Chemistry and Physics** 2021, 274, 125105.  
DOI: **10.1016/j.matchemphys.2021.125105**

**Directly supports:** Mg-containing samples retain structure better; Mg-free composition reaches higher capacity but has poorer structural integrity.  
**Role:** strong precedent for the present capacity–stability trade-off.

### [10] Atomic/nanoscale cation synergy during conversion

**Wang, K.; Hua, W.; Huang, X.; Stenzel, D.; Wang, J.; Ding, Z.; Cui, Y.; Wang, Q.; Ehrenberg, H.; Breitung, B.; Kübel, C.; Mu, X.**  
*Synergy of cations in high entropy oxide lithium ion battery anode.*  
**Nature Communications** 2023, 14, 1487.  
DOI: **10.1038/s41467-023-37034-6**

**Directly supports:** an electrochemically inactive cation stabilizes an oxide nanophase during conversion while other cations form a metallic network.  
**Role:** provides a mechanistic basis for describing Mg-like inactive cation roles as structural stabilization rather than faster Li diffusion.

### [11] Mg-containing spinel and milling-induced spinel/rock-salt change

**Zheng, Y.; Wu, X.; Lan, X.; Hu, R.**  
*A Spinel (FeNiCrMnMgAl)3O4 High Entropy Oxide as a Cycling Stable Anode Material for Li-Ion Batteries.*  
**Processes** 2022, 10, 49.  
DOI: **10.3390/pr10010049**

**Directly supports:** reversible spinel-to-rock-salt phase evolution and a similar structural change induced by high-energy ball milling in a Mg-containing spinel HEO.  
**Role:** bridge reference supporting both the Mg and ball-milling discussions.

## E. GITT / current interruption / phase-transition overpotential

### [15] GITT assumptions and pitfalls

**Jia, M.; Zhang, W.; Cai, X.; Zhan, X.; Hou, L.; Yuan, C.; Guo, Z.**  
*Re-understanding the galvanostatic intermittent titration technique: Pitfalls in evaluation of diffusion coefficients and rational suggestions.*  
**Journal of Power Sources** 2022, 543, 231843.  
DOI: **10.1016/j.jpowsour.2022.231843**

**Directly supports:** GITT-derived D is assumption- and parameter-sensitive; inappropriate models/time windows can cause major errors.  
**Role:** methodological basis for not centering the present conversion electrode manuscript on one apparent D value.

### [16] Intermittent current interruption / E vs sqrt(t)

**Chien, Y.-C.; Liu, H.; Menon, A. S.; Brant, W. R.; Brandell, D.; Lacey, M. J.**  
*Rapid determination of solid-state diffusion coefficients in Li-based batteries via intermittent current interruption method.*  
**Nature Communications** 2023, 14, 2289.  
DOI: **10.1038/s41467-023-37989-6**

**Directly supports:** early current-off response and sqrt(t)-based current-interruption analysis under the semi-infinite diffusion regime.  
**Present manuscript boundary:** use the t→0 intercept as an apparent fast current-off descriptor; do not assign the sqrt(t) term uniquely to intrinsic bulk diffusion in a phase-evolving conversion electrode.

### [17] Nucleation contribution to phase-transition overpotential

**Komayko, A. I.; Nazarov, E. E.; Tyablikov, O. A.; Fedotov, S. S.; Antipov, E. V.; Nikitina, V. A.**  
*Unraveling the contribution of nucleation to the intercalation energy barrier for phase-transforming Li-ion battery materials.*  
**Journal of Power Sources** 2024, 624, 235589.  
DOI: **10.1016/j.jpowsour.2024.235589**

**Directly supports:** nucleation can provide a significant material-level contribution to overpotential in phase-transforming electrodes.  
**Boundary:** not a conversion HEO study; use for general phase-transition overpotential physics only.

# F. Particle-size / coherent-nucleation references

### [18] Coherency strain and nanoparticle phase separation

**Cogswell, D. A.; Bazant, M. Z.**  
*Coherency Strain and the Kinetics of Phase Separation in LiFePO4 Nanoparticles.*  
**ACS Nano** 2012, 6, 2215–2225.  
DOI: **10.1021/nn204177u**

**Directly supports:** coherency strain strongly modifies nanoscale phase-separation thermodynamics/kinetics.  
**Boundary:** LiFePO4, not a conversion HEO; use only for general nanoscale phase-transition physics.

### [19] Surface-to-volume control of coherent nucleation barrier

**Cogswell, D. A.; Bazant, M. Z.**  
*Theory of Coherent Nucleation in Phase-Separating Nanoparticles.*  
**Nano Letters** 2013, 13, 3036–3041.  
DOI: **10.1021/nl400497t**

**Directly supports:** the coherent nucleation barrier decreases with increasing area-to-volume ratio and can vanish below a critical size because surface wetting/elastic relaxation changes the balance of chemical and elastic energy.  
**Role:** general physical precedent showing that nanosizing need not increase phase-transition overpotential.

### [20] Direct nanosize effect in spinel-type HEO

**Li, K.; Shi, L.; An, J.; Zhang, M.; Du, Y.; Ma, Y.; Lou, S.; Yin, G.; Yu, Z.; Hua, X.; Huo, H.**  
*Stabilizing Configurational Entropy in Spinel-type High Entropy Oxides during Discharge–Charge by Overcoming Kinetic Sluggish Diffusion.*  
**Angewandte Chemie International Edition** 2025, 64, e202518569.  
DOI: **10.1002/anie.202518569**

**Directly supports:** comparison of ~150 nm and ~15 nm spinel-type HEOs; the nanosized material shortens solid-state diffusion lengths, reduces polarization, changes plateau shape, and enables more complete/reversible conversion-type phase evolution.  
**Role:** closest literature precedent for interpreting lower BM-HEO transition polarization despite microstructural refinement.  
**Boundary:** do not equate the present ~2 μm BSE aggregates with the ~15 nm primary particles in this paper; use as mechanistic precedent for effective-domain/interface refinement only.

# H. Literature gaps that define the novelty of the present manuscript

1. **Same-family HEO/BM comparisons exist**, including conventional GITT analysis [6], but they largely compress kinetics into apparent D or compact resistance/diffusion descriptors.
2. **Phase-transition pathways are now structurally established** [5,7], but their state-resolved polarization/relaxation signature under GITT has not been used to distinguish processing and compositional effects in a 2 × 2 comparison.
3. **Mg stabilization is established in conversion-type HEOs** [8–10], but the present data add the specific observation that polarization amplitude decreases while characteristic relaxation time does not accelerate.
4. **Ball milling/fragmentation can increase utilization** [11,12], but the present data separate this utilization gain from long-rest relaxation and quantify broadening of the transition-associated polarization feature.
5. The manuscript's central contribution is therefore not a new GITT algorithm; it is the **mechanistic separation of electrochemical accessibility, transformation extent, and relaxation time as independently synthesis-sensitive coordinates**.

# I. Reference-management rules for this project

- Verify DOI/title/volume/article/page against publisher or authoritative institutional metadata before final submission export.
- Prefer [7] over reviews when claiming the exact spinel→rock-salt pathway.
- Prefer [9] and [10] when discussing Mg stabilization; explicitly state that those systems are rock-salt HEOs and therefore provide mechanistic precedent rather than direct crystallographic proof for the present spinel.
- Use [11] for the statement that high-energy milling can itself induce a spinel/rock-salt-like transformation.
- Use [12] for fragmentation-driven conversion/interfacial utilization in the same FeCoNiCrMn family.
- Keep [15]–[17] method/physics references separate from material-specific structural evidence.
- Use [18] and [19] only for general coherent-nucleation/size physics; they do not prove the mechanism in the present HEO.
- Use [20] as the closest direct spinel-HEO nanosize precedent, while explicitly distinguishing its ~15 nm primary particles from the present ball-milled aggregate morphology.
- Do not cite a paper for a statement stronger than what the paper directly establishes.
