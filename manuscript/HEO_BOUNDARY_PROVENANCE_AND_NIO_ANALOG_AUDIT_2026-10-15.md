## KEY CONVERSION PRECEDENT FOUND IN THE RE-SEARCH
- **Ng, B.; Faegh, E.; Lateef, S.; Karakalos, S. G.; Mustain, W. E.** “Structure and chemistry of the solid electrolyte interphase (SEI) on a high capacity conversion-based anode: NiO,” *J. Mater. Chem. A* **2021**, **9**, 523–537, DOI **10.1039/D0TA09683K**, [full text](https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlehtml/2021/ta/d0ta09683k). Exact Figure 3 defines **FOUR I–IV electrochemical/SOC regions** from GITT-derived apparent diffusion S²D, apparent exchange current, OCP. Boundary voltage and charge:
  - I OCP > 1 V, Q < 72 mAh/g approx;
  - II 0.7 < OCP < 1 V, 72 < Q < 610 mAh/g;
  - III 0.5 < OCP < 0.7 V, 610 < Q < 790 mAh/g;
  - IV 0 < OCP < 0.5 V, Q > 790 mAh/g.
  - Regions II–III predominantly NiO conversion; I and IV SEI important; Figure 4 fits separate electrochemical kinetics (BV/MHC αeff) in each. Therefore **there IS an explicit prior instance of electrochemically subdividing a conversion oxide into four SOC intervals and assigning dominant mechanisms.** Earlier literature audit omission now corrected. DO NOT claim no such work. Distinctive HEO method is **ΔErest/t63 without forcing diffusion-model interpretation + crossed four-sample modification-specific region charge changes**, not first four-region partition ever.

- **Lowe et al.**, *J. Mater. Chem. A* 2013 DOI 10.1039/C2TA01270G: Mn3O4 operando XAS/XRD assigns electrochemical regions to LiMn3O4, MnO, Mn, non-metal centered reactions.
