# HEO SI v11 — Batch 4 Four-Step Homogeneous Microkinetic Assets

**Date:** 2026-09-29

## Current model

The obsolete three-step / heterogeneous-accessibility model from SI v10 is removed from the current manuscript evidence chain.

The current homogeneous four-step effective sequence is:
O <-> I <-> J <-> K <-> C

- R1: initial electrochemical lithiation/electron transfer
- R2: effective M–O dissociation/local reconstruction
- R3: effective Li2O-forming/product-side reconstruction
- R4: subsequent electron transfer/metal reduction

The states are effective kinetic states, not uniquely identified HEO phases.

## Main physical result

Changing one internal step alone gives the conventional response: slowing the step decreases cutoff capacity and lengthens relaxation.

Step-selective changes in R2 and R3 generate a finite homogeneous parameter region in which both Q_cutoff/Q0 > 1 and t63/t63,0 > 1. This is the microkinetic existence proof used in the main manuscript.

The model is not a quantitative fit to BM-HEO or Mg-HEO. Mg is retained as a complementary experimental constraint in observable space; no unique model parameter is assigned to Mg incorporation.

The experimental full relaxation-voltage magnitude is not a quantitative target of this coarse-grained conversion model.
