## Exact mechanism direction
- Main measured finding: Mg affects first-lithiation nominal GITT R3 substantially more than R2, **R2 −5.3% vs R3 −51.6%** (unmilled pair), and **R2 −10.3% vs R3 −77.3%** (milled pair). These numbers are region pulse-charge contrasts, NOT GCD.
- **Preferred, still unproven explanation:** Mg behaves as a comparatively redox-inactive structural component and supports persistence of Mg-containing oxide environments, making later conversion less accessible under the experimental potential window. Do not claim direct detection of MgO, a particular Mg site, specific oxide-stabilization free energy, or the resulting metal/Li2O product fraction.
- **Concurrent alternatives:** redox-active metal inventory reduction, selective replacement of a metal whose reduction contributes to R3, shifted equilibrium/reaction voltage, kinetic/polarization limitations near cutoff, and interface/phase effects. Approximately uniform dilution alone does not reproduce extreme R3/R2 disproportion.
- t63 characterizes **post-pulse voltage recovery**, not current-on reaction speed; weak remaining R3 t63 shift cannot rule out kinetic conversion limitation.
- Figure2 BET source values: HEO 3.94, BM 18.159, Mg 6.49, BM-Mg 16.64 m²/g; Mg has higher BET than parent in unmilled pair, so decreased gas-accessible surface alone is insufficient to explain the R3 change.
