- The nominal dQ/dV–GITT peak differences are within 32 mV.
- HEO and Mg-HEO have invariant GITT peak states across the 105 tested definitions.
- BM-HEO remains in a narrow higher-potential band.
- BM-Mg-HEO has a broader peak-position and width sensitivity because its feature is shallow.
- Cycle history changes the excess-response amplitude/state location more strongly than t63.
- The cycle-history result is retained as robustness evidence in SI, not a separate main-text headline.
