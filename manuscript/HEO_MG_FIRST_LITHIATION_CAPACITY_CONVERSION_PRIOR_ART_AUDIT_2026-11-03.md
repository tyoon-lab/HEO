# Mg incorporation in conversion HEO anodes — first-lithiation capacity vs cycling stability (primary-literature audit, 2026-11-03)

**PI's explicit correction:** *initial lithiation capacity*, *reversible first charge capacity*, *capacity after cycling*, and *capacity retention* are distinct observables. Never support a first-lithiation capacity assertion solely by reporting cycling stability. This document checks Mg composition, initial capacity, reduction chemistry and same-composition/matched material significance.

## 1. Direct Mg-content comparison in rocksalt HEO: support for initial capacity decrease (but conference proceedings)

**Lökçü, Özden, Anık.** *Effect of Mg Content on the Electrochemical Properties of (MgCoNiZnLi)O Based High Entropy Oxides for Li-Ion Batteries*. *The Eurasia Proceedings of Science Technology Engineering and Mathematics* (2020), 11, 53–56, https://dergipark.org.tr/en/pub/epstem/article/836327 . Primary full text https://dergipark.org.tr/tr/download/article-file/1432632 .

- Nominal rocksalt `Mg5(CoNiZnLi)95O` vs `Mg35(CoNiZnLi)65O` (percent of metal sublattice). Both single phase by XRD. Other cation fractions necessarily change.
- At 100 mA/g, 0.01–3.0 V versus Li: **initial discharge capacity 1459 vs 1222 mAh/g** (Mg 5% vs 35%); **first discharge plateau 0.75 vs 0.32 V**. Hence +Mg fraction correlates with 237 mAh/g lower first lithiation capacity (16.2% relative to 1459).
- By fifth cycle: 168 vs 576 mAh/g, so capacity after cycling trends oppositely. These are **not** initial capacity evidence. Authors attribute to inactive MgO and stability.
- Strength: same rock-salt composition family with direct initial cycle controlled variation; weakness: conference (4 pages), rocksalt not our spinel, very high initial discharge includes SEI/irreversible contributions, cation dilution is confounded with changes in specific reaction completeness. Do not cite as direct proof that Mg inhibits a particular conversion step.

## 2. Spinel composition substitution and capacity

**Minouei et al.** *Tuning the electrochemical performance of high-entropy oxide nanopowder for anode Li-ion storage via structural tailoring*. *Journal of Power Sources* 549 (2022) 232041 DOI https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpowsour.2022.232041 .
- Compares nanocrystalline (CrMnFeCoNi)3O4 and (MgCrMnFeCo)3O4, among others; Ni replaced with Mg, with structures examined. Abstract specifically says Ni presence favors higher capacity, whereas Mg replacement improves stability and maintains high capacity after 1000 cycles.
- Important: *"higher capacity" associated with Ni* is relevant to capacity, but abstract doesn't supply **comparable first-lithiation numbers**, so full Fig profiles are needed before using as hard quantitative first-discharge proof. The post-1000-cycle result is a different metric.

**Patra et al.** *Effects of Elemental Modulation on Phase Purity and Electrochemical Properties of Co-free High-Entropy Spinel Oxide Anodes for Lithium-Ion Batteries*. *Advanced Functional Materials* (2022) DOI https://doi.org/10.1002/adfm.202110992 .
- Starting (CrNiMnFe)3O4, fifth element Cu/Mg/V. Composition affects single-phase purity and charge/discharge performance; Cu containing system is preferred for phase purity/high-rate/reversibility. Their abstract explicitly rejects the notion that an inactive spectator is necessary for high cyclability. Useful for *Mg as tunable composition*; phase purity differences are confounders and abstract doesn't support a universal first-discharge decrease for Mg.

**Xiao et al.** *Enhanced Li-Ion Diffusion and Cycling Stability of Ni-Free High-Entropy Spinel Oxide Anodes with High-Concentration Oxygen Vacancies.* *ACS Applied Materials & Interfaces* (2023) DOI https://doi.org/10.1021/acsami.2c12374 .
- (FeCoCrMnZn)3O4 vs (FeCoCrMnMg)3O4, both prepared by ball milling; XPS oxygen vacancies 42.7 vs42.5% initially; Zn variant higher GITT apparent diffusivity while cycling and high-rate capacity favorable. Matched Zn-for-Mg comparison, not an Mg-addition vs pristine fifth-metal constant. Its **828.6 mAh/g at 2.0 A/g after 2000 cycles is a cycling capacity, not an initial lithiation value**. Original publication likely relevant to Fig 3 first-cycle profiles but abstract cannot establish their initial numerical disparity.

**Nam et al.** *High-Entropy Spinel Oxide Ferrites for Battery Applications.* *Chemistry of Materials* 36 (2024), 4481–4494 DOI https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.chemmater.4c00085 ; PMC https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11099913/ . Four spinel ferrites with varied constituents incl Mg. First discharge capacities among HESO compositions 972–1028 mAh/g; 68–76% of calculated capacity upon first charge, with 1st discharge exceeding theoretical because of electrolyte decomposition/SEI. XAS at selected end states finds Fe/Co/Ni/Cu nearly elemental after discharge while Mn remains ~2+; first charge primarily reoxidizes Fe. Critically, Mg-bearing compositions may still give large initial capacity; Mg alone does not imply a universal large penalty. XAS data do not quantify Mg reduction (Mg K-edge not treated as the relevant TM edges). Compare stoichiometry carefully before inferring an isolated Mg effect.

## 3. Physically resolved oxide framework / mechanism

**Wang et al.** *Synergy of cations in high entropy oxide lithium ion battery anode*. *Nature Communications* 14 (2023) 1487 DOI https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-023-37034-6 . Operando/endpoint XAS and electron microscopy on rock-salt (MgCoNiCuZn)O after FIRST lithiation: Co/Ni/Cu/Zn reduced to metallic or LiZn phases, **Mg and oxygen retained in the residual oxide matrix** closely intertwined with metal network. This provides strong structural evidence for **Mg-rich residual oxide versus active TM reduction** in that compound. **No pristine Mg-free matched sample**, no assignment of suppression specifically to R3 of our spinel. Strong mechanistic analogy, not direct phase proof for our chemistry.

**Liu et al.** *Deciphering functional differentiation of elements in high-entropy spinel oxides as ultralong-life anodes in lithium-ion batteries.* *Chemical Science* 17 (2026) 6116–6124 DOI https://doi.org/10.1039/D5SC10185A . CoCuMgCrFe spinel. DFT + experiments describe Cr/Co/Fe as conversion-active, Mg/Cu as more structural elements suppressing volume expansion; active sites help Li2O decomposition. The evidence for Mg's stabilizer role is largely model and cycling behavior (Mg exclusion worsens retention); **not a direct Mg-induced initial lithiation capacity loss**. Cite for *stabilization hypothesis*, not first-capacity direction.

**Petrus et al.** *High-Entropy Sn0.8(Co0.2Mg0.2Mn0.2Ni0.2Zn0.2)2.2O4 Conversion–Alloying Anode Material for Li-Ion Cells: Altered Lithium Storage Mechanism, Activation of Mg, and Origins of the Improved Cycling Stability*. *ACS Applied Materials & Interfaces* (2022) DOI https://doi.org/10.1021/acsami.2c11038 . Sn-based conversion/alloying spinel with evidence authors describe as Mg electrochemical activation; warns that Mg is not universally inert in all HEO oxide contexts. Very different material; do not transfer directly to our FeCoCrNiMn spinel.

## 4. BM and size prior art

**Zhai et al.** *Insight of the evolution of structure and energy storage mechanism of (FeCoNiCrMn)3O4 spinel high entropy oxide in life-cycle span as lithium-ion battery anode*. *Journal of Power Sources* 603 (2024) 234418 DOI https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpowsour.2024.234418 . Fragmentation and high-energy ball milling + FEC experiments link refined particle structure with enhanced conversion reversibility and interfacial capacity, but paper's strongest numeric example is **cycle 825 (2831 mAh/g)**. This is NOT direct proof ball milling raises first-lithiation capacity in every HEO.
**Li et al.** *Stabilizing Configurational Entropy in Spinel-type High Entropy Oxides during Discharge–Charge by Overcoming Kinetic Sluggish Diffusion*. *Angew Chem Int Ed* (2025) DOI https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.202518569 . Controlled particle size influences conversion completion in spinel HEO. Useful mechanism-to-size link, but not necessarily identical to BM (BM also changes microstrain/disorder/surface reactions).
**Chen et al.** *A new spinel ... (MgTiZnCuFe)3O4 with fast reaction kinetics...* *RSC Advances* 2020 DOI https://doi.org/10.1039/D0RA00255K . High-energy ball milling used in sample synthesis and refinement, not a documented controlled BM vs no BM first-cycle pair. Do not cite to claim universal capacity boost from BM.

## 5. Manuscript recommendation

- Present **BM as morphology/particle-accessibility perturbation** and **Mg as a cation-composition perturbation that modifies redox-active-ion content and may influence residual oxide conversion/stability**. Direct pristine-vs-Mg and with/without milling capacity signs belong to OUR matched first-lithiation experiments, not to universal prior art.
- Distinguish strong evidence: 2020 Lokcu Mg content 1459→1222 initially (rocksalt conference, directly relevant signed effect), 2022 Minouei Ni more favorable for capacity than Mg-substituted spinel (qualitative abstract), 2024 Nam spinel HESO capacities remain high despite Mg; 2023 Wang elemental Mg-rich residual oxide; 2026 Liu stabilizer role is NOT first-lithiation proof.
- **First discharge GCD includes SEI formation and irreversible charge**, so Mg-induced first-lithiation charge decrease cannot by itself be equated with less conversion, nor does a nominal R3 GITT reduction establish a specific Mg-containing oxide phase. Our strongest novel finding is *the electrochemical-region localization of the first-lithiation charge difference* rather than structurally verified phase-blocking.
- Suggested concise P4 pair of literature-grounded sentences (citation numbers to assign at final bibliography revision):
  > Ball milling was selected to vary particle morphology and surface accessibility, whereas Mg incorporation was selected to modify the cation composition and the balance of conversion-active and comparatively stable oxide components. Previous studies show that particle refinement can change the extent of HEO conversion, while Mg incorporation can modify first-lithiation capacity and favor Mg-rich oxide domains during conversion [Li 2025; Lökçü 2020; Wang 2023].
- Better not use Liu 2026 cycle-stability data to justify initial charge decrease. 2020 proceedings rock-salt and 2023 Nat Commun rock-salt are analogs, 2022 Minouei and Xiao 2023 are spinel context. Consider word economy in Intro; more detailed literature analogy fits R3 Discussion.
