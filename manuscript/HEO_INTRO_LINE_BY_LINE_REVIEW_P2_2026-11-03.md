## Suggested revised P2 (proposed, not yet incorporated in full Introduction)

This progression raises an important question when HEO materials are modified: where along lithiation does the resulting capacity change arise? Particle-size control, cation substitution, and Li₂O incorporation have been shown to alter spinel HEO capacity and conversion behavior [9,20,24]. These studies have examined intermediate oxide conversion, ion transport, and voltage features associated with insertion and conversion. The charge gained or lost over different portions of lithiation, however, remains less well quantified. A modification may influence charge storage over the full lithiation range or preferentially affect particular portions, potentially with changes of opposite sign. Locating and quantifying these contributions would clarify how processing and composition alter the conversion response and support targeted materials design.
