## Overall finding
**Already well established**: (1) segmentation of lithiation voltage or dQ/dV into multiple regions, integration of region-specific charge, relation to capacity fade or processing changes; (2) GITT used in metal oxide conversion to distinguish initial intercalation from conversion; (3) operando XRD/TEM/XAS assigning electrochemical features to distinct intermediate oxides and metal/Li2O; (4) GITT for HEO comparison and even exactly 10 min pulse + 60 min relaxation in Mg-containing spinel composition. Consequently neither “region-specific charge” nor “GITT of HEO conversion anode” is first-in-field.
**Unverified potentially defensible novelty**: using **finite-window ΔErest and operational t63 plus rest-end voltage** as coupled, diffusion-coefficient-free diagnostics of changing dominant reaction behavior **AND** spatial/electrochemical localization of *opposing material modification effects* (milling vs Mg) in the matched four-composition design. Search did not establish prior identical joint method, but not proof of worldwide absence. Must demonstrate that GITT relaxation supplies non-redundant information relative to standard dQ/dV/voltage peak segmentation.

## Tier 1: Silicon and SiO precedent
1. **T. Yoon; C. C. Nguyen; D. M. Seo; B. L. Lucht, “Capacity Fading Mechanisms of Silicon Nanoparticle Negative Electrodes for Lithium Ion Batteries,” *J. Electrochem. Soc.* 2015, 162, A2325–A2330. DOI: 10.1149/2.0731512jes.** Voltage-profile comparison over cycling: largest evolution at early lithiation (>0.16 V vs Li); incomplete delithiation from rising contact and SEI resistances; cutoff voltage and electrolyte additive change retention. [Publisher/institutional repository](https://digitalcommons.uri.edu/chm_facpubs/133/). Referenced as ref.11 by Zhang 2018. **Direct intellectual precedent for segment-localization of fading and mechanistic interpretation**.
2. **L. Zhang; Y. Qin; Y. Liu; Q. Liu; Y. Ren; A. N. Jansen; W. Lu, “Capacity Fading Mechanism and Improvement of Cycling Stability of the SiO Anode for Lithium-Ion Batteries,” *J. Electrochem. Soc.* 2018, 165, A2102–A2107. DOI 10.1149/2.0431810jes.** [OA published PDF](https://scholars.cityu.edu.hk/ws/portalfiles/portal/34818313/35.pdf). **Figure 2(b-d), Figure S2, S4:** explicitly define lithium-voltage L1=0.8–0.27 V, L2=0.27–0.16 V, L3=0.16–0.01 V, integrate dQ/dV per window and trace contributions over cycles. Fast initial fade localized L1, long-term fade grows over L2/L3; electrochemical contrast among binder/additive and annealing. Note this is SiO alloy/carbonate matrix, NOT conversion TMO. Strongest closest methodological precedent. PDF text directly checked; PDF screenshot retrieval failed (cache miss), so figure inspection only from text/captions.
3. Other Si references: *Scientific Reports* 2016, silicon-graphene composites with dQ/dV phase signatures in different cycles DOI 10.1038/srep37787. Si c-Li15Si4 transition, cutoff dependence shown extensively (e.g. 2020 Nanotechnology review DOI 10.1088/1361-6528/ABB850). These are supporting, not priority references.

## Tier 2: Li-rich layered cathodes
4. **E.-S. Lee; A. Manthiram**, “Smart design of lithium-rich layered oxide cathode compositions with suppressed voltage decay,” *J. Mater. Chem. A* 2014, DOI **10.1039/C3TA14975G**. Compares high-voltage activation plateau length with long-term voltage fade and dQ/dV peak evolution; phase transformation to lower-voltage spinel-like response. Independent composition comparison and region-specific performance.
5. **K. Kleiner et al.**, “Origin of High Capacity and Poor Cycling Stability of Li-Rich Layered Oxides: A Long-Duration In Situ Synchrotron Powder Diffraction Study,” *Chem. Mater.* 2018, 30, 3656–3667, DOI **10.1021/acs.chemmater.8b00163**. dQ/dV across ~100 cycles with operando synchrotron diffraction reveals transition-metal migration; strong example of claiming mechanism only with structural correlation.
6. **M. Merz; B. Ying; P. Nagel; S. Schuppler; K. Kleiner**, “Reversible and Irreversible Redox Processes in Li-Rich Layered Oxides,” *Chem. Mater.* 2021, 33, 9534–9545, DOI **10.1021/acs.chemmater.1c02573**. dQ/dV at 1,2,10,20,50,100 cycles; O (~4.5 V activation) vs Ni/Co/Mn redox with NEXAFS, MS and operando SXRD. 7. **Li-rich pouch cell RSC Advances 2024**, “New information about the cyclable capacity fading process of a pouch cell with Li-rich layered oxide cathodes,” DOI **10.1039/D4RA02472A**. dQ/dV reduction peaks P1 oxygen, P2 Ni+Co, P3 Mn/structural transformation, plus EIS/polarization and quantitative early/mid/late cycling fade attribution. Note early/mid/late refer **cycles**, peaks refer voltage domain.

## Tier 3: conversion and HEO direct precedents — priority
9. **M. A. Lowe; J. Gao; H. D. Abruña**, “In operando X-ray studies of the conversion reaction in Mn3O4 lithium battery anodes,” *J. Mater. Chem. A* 2013, 1, 2094–2103, DOI **10.1039/C2TA01270G**. ## Diagnostic/experimental reviewer threats
- The four R1-R4 are electrochemical *regions*, not four chemical conversion reactions or proven 4 in situ phases. Conversion metal/Li2O product production can overlap R2/R3.
- The measured `t63` is post-current *voltage relaxation* rate, **not actual metal reduction kinetics** and `ΔErest` mixes kinetically and thermodynamically impacted voltage–composition curvature.
- **Nominal regional GITT capacity is pulse count × current × pulse duration / mass**. Segmentation uses GITT rest and t63, but **capacities alone are not a new mathematical information source**; do not label regional allocation “reconstructed” or “phase yield”. GCD capacities are independent data and not directly decomposed.
- **Potential confound:** a stage boundary moving because of material modification can shift charge counted from R2 to R3. Mg could shift conversion/polarization beyond cutoff. Need robustness against variable boundaries, raw peak shapes and equal-potential windows.
- **Critical uniqueness test:** compare (A) fixed voltage or dQ/dV landmark-only staging versus (B) rest-voltage-only versus (C) voltage+ΔErest+t63 staging on the **same four samples**; quantify whether GITT relaxation is actually needed to distinguish BM-R2 / Mg-R3 and captures anything different. If dQ/dV alone reproduces everything, frame GITT-specific novelty more modestly, emphasize simultaneous characterization of relaxation and charge-accessibility.
- **Boundary sensitivity:** vary L1/L3/L4 ±one/two GITT pulses, report R2 + and R3 - pattern persistence for both milling/composition matched comparisons. Original ΔQ nominal multiples 16.7 mAh/g; small partition shifts can change regional amounts.
- Additional minimal controls: plot dQ/dV/ dQ/dErest vs Erest; full voltage profile pulse/rest, ΔErest/t63 vs comparable *absolute potential* and *normalized charge* not only per-sample normalized SOC; keep marked regions and measurement cutoffs. Most reviewer-proof SI includes transparent stage-boundary algorithm/landmarks and baseline ablation.
- For **mechanistic Mg attribution**, priority is state-resolved XAS at 2-3 representative samples/positions (R2/R3 ends) or in situ XRD/TEM and compositional inventory; chemistry hypotheses not proven by voltage recovery. The minimal doable contribution from existing data is more cautious R2/R3 literature-guided association.
- 2015 Yoon *JES* paper is actually an extremely relevant historical precedent and referenced by 2018 Zhang *JES*; acknowledge rather than claim region-of-loss discovery.
- Further search did **not verify an identical** four-material GITT rest ΔErest/t63 charge localization study; this absence is search-limited, not proof no such paper.

## Implication for Abstract & Intro
Kept latest capacity-change-first narrative currently valid as working text. Avoid “first to distinguish electrochemical reaction stages,” “GITT reveals metal-specific phase transitions,” “four intrinsic conversion steps,” or “BM faster relaxation = faster Li diffusion.” Position experimental advantage as **multiple electrochemical observables exposing where processing vs composition affect charge accommodation and post-pulse equilibration**, not as the mere invention of stage partitioning. Consider one sentence in Introduction acknowledging prior dQ/dV segmentation in Si and Li-rich oxides and GITT+operando multi-oxide literature, then the remaining unaddressed contrast: difference between stage-localized *accessible charge* and *relaxation response* in paired HEO modifications.
