# HEO Abstract — opening updated following PI discussion (2026-10-15)

**Status:** Working candidate, NOT a PI-locked or inserted Word Abstract. The PI accepted a progression-based opening to avoid implying that metal/Li₂O formation only starts after R2. The cycle-specific quantitative stage analysis remains based on first-lithiation GITT, and the final choice to include later GITT cycles remains open.

## Abstract candidate

Lithiation of spinel high-entropy oxide (HEO) anodes proceeds through multiple reaction stages involving initial Li uptake and progressive conversion toward metal/Li₂O products. Because these overlapping processes contribute charge over an extended lithiation interval, changes in overall capacity provide limited information about which stages are affected by material modifications. Ball milling and Mg incorporation produce opposing changes in lithiation capacity, motivating an examination of their stage-dependent effects. Analysis of galvanostatic intermittent titration (GITT) voltage and post-pulse relaxation identifies four electrochemically distinct regimes: early lithiation, conversion onset and progression, late conversion, and post-plateau deep lithiation. Ball milling produces its largest positive regional capacity change during conversion onset and progression, associated with oxide destabilization and reconstruction. In contrast, Mg incorporation produces its largest negative regional change during later conversion, associated with further reduction and evolution toward metal/Li₂O-derived products. Increasing BET surface area after milling is consistent with improved access to reactive oxide domains, whereas the Mg-associated late-conversion loss is consistent with reduced accessibility of later conversion states, possibly involving persistent Mg-containing oxide environments. These findings locate the dominant reaction-stage origins of capacity gains and losses, providing a basis for evaluating the physical effects of material modifications on conversion-type anodes.

**Keywords:** high-entropy oxide; conversion anode; GITT; voltage relaxation; reaction-stage origin; ball milling; Mg incorporation

## Interpretation gates
- `Progressive conversion toward metal/Li₂O` does **not** imply products are absent during R2; they can begin to appear in R2 and continue through R3.
- `multiple reaction stages` refers to changing dominant electrochemical behavior, not five independent metal conversion steps, four pure phases, or direct GITT phase identification.
- The large regional BM-R2 and Mg-R3 nominal charge contrasts are verified only for first-lithiation GITT, not for later cycles or independent GCD discharge capacities.
- Mg-rich persistent oxide remains the leading **hypothesis**, not a detected product or proven Mg lattice site.
