## What is now integrated (changes from v24)
1. **Introduction:** explicitly distinguishes reduction/conversion on lithiation from reoxidation/reconversion on delithiation. The multistage **electrochemical profile** reflects overlapping processes, not necessarily independent conversion of five cations. Source/citation verification remains open.
2. **Section 2.3:** integrates the proposed evidence chain from pulse/relaxation voltage and t63 to L1/L3/L4 landmarks to dominant physical processes in R1–R4. R2 associates with oxide destabilization/reorganization and reduction; R3 with further reduction toward metal/Li₂O-derived products. This is the paper's central mechanistic interpretation. The claim stops short of direct species/phase identification by GITT and does not infer a microscopic diffusion rate directly from t63.
3. **Section 2.4:** BM R2 gain interpreted in terms of greater participation/accessibility of oxide domains at conversion onset, possibly aided by milling-induced smaller particles, disorder and new interfaces; reconciles R2 higher nominal charge with the observed longer R2–R4 relaxation times. Secondary R3 negative and R4 positive contributions are acknowledged.
4. **Section 2.5:** preferential Mg-related R3 decrease interpreted as modified accessibility/energetics of later conversion, rather than solely as a cation inventory/dilution effect. Conditional possibilities include altered local environment/structural stability. No unverified claim of Mg lattice site, metal–oxygen bond strength, or reduced Li₂O yield is inserted.
5. **Section 2.6 and Conclusion:** connect stage-localized capacity gains and losses with plausible physical origins, distinguishing charge participation from relaxation persistence. Four-sample directionality retained.
6. **Figure 4 caption:** stage labels treated as physically interpreted dominant processes, while avoiding a claim of individual phase identification.
