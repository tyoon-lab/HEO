# Literature Positioning Notes for the Electrochemistry Discussion

**Date:** 2026-09-18

## Purpose

Use existing literature to support a mechanistic interpretation without claiming that the manuscript introduces a new GITT inversion method.

## Phase-transition interpretation

Relevant literature categories to cite in the final manuscript:

1. Compositionally related spinel HEO anodes showing lithiation-induced spinel-to-rock-salt / conversion-type phase evolution by in-situ or ex-situ structural analysis.
2. Phase-transforming battery electrodes showing that nucleation, phase-boundary propagation, strain accommodation, and structural reorganization contribute additional polarization / hysteresis.
3. Mg-containing or redox-inactive-component HEO studies discussing stabilization / spectator / buffering roles during conversion.
4. Ball-milling studies showing disorder, lattice perturbation, or mechanically induced changes in spinel/rock-salt phase tendency.

Manuscript positioning:

- Do not claim that GITT alone proves a phase transition.
- Assign the late-stage feature primarily to the established spinel-to-rock-salt/conversion process based on its voltage/state location and its systematic response to Mg and ball milling.
- Use `assigned primarily to`, `consistent with`, and `predominantly suppresses` rather than claiming direct structural observation in the present experiment.

## GITT / current-interruption positioning

The current-off analysis should be positioned as use of established electrochemical transient concepts:

- current-interruption / ICI-style separation of an intercept-like instantaneous response and a sqrt(t)-dependent early relaxation;
- conventional GITT apparent diffusivity retained only as a familiar comparator;
- model-free finite-window relaxation descriptors used to preserve information that one scalar D does not contain.

Do not market t50/t63/t90 as a new inversion method. They are simple model-free descriptors of the observed relaxation trajectory.

## Distributed-response positioning

The separate `D-only -> D + compact relaxation -> distributed relaxation` audit should be framed as established model hierarchy / identifiability analysis if it is ever shown.

Important literature-compatible points:

- finite diffusion itself has an eigenmode/exponential-series representation;
- an extra RC-like exponential can overlap diffusion modes;
- excellent fit quality does not guarantee a unique mechanistic parameter assignment;
- time-domain DRT / pulse-response decompositions already exist in the literature.

Therefore the current HEO manuscript should not claim invention of a new GITT method. Its contribution is the electrochemical interpretation of synthesis-controlled phase evolution in the HEO system.
