# HEO / GITT Cross-Paper Data-Use Boundary — 2026-10-12

## Decision

The separate GITT/apparent-diffusivity manuscript may use **pristine HEO first-lithiation GITT** as a generalization case for multistep reconstructive conversion.

It should **not** use the BM-HEO, Mg-HEO, or BM-Mg-HEO modification comparisons as its central HEO result.

## Division of scientific ownership

### HEO materials manuscript
Owns:
- ball-milling capacity gain;
- Mg-induced capacity loss;
- R1–R4 stage localization;
- BM effect concentrated mainly in R2;
- Mg effect concentrated mainly in R3;
- materials/process/composition interpretation.

### GITT relaxation manuscript
May use pristine HEO to show:
- post-pulse relaxation changes across successive regions/stages of reconstructive conversion;
- the relaxation response can be represented on a reaction-progress × characteristic-time map;
- reconstructive conversion provides a third non-ideal reaction class alongside LFP two-phase reaction and Li2MnO3 activation.

## Guardrail

Avoid duplicate figures and avoid presenting the HEO manuscript's BM/Mg stage-origin result as a new result in the GITT paper.

The existing HEO/BM D_app method-dependence and ranking-reversal audits remain valid internal/background material but are not the preferred cross-paper data-use route.

This boundary preserves the HEO paper as a materials/conversion paper while allowing the pristine HEO dataset to support generality of the separate GITT relaxation concept.
