# Conversion Microkinetics — Literature Map, 2026-09-23

## Purpose

Track prior work relevant to treating conversion reactions as a multi-step electrochemical kinetic network.

This is a working literature map, not yet the final manuscript reference list. Verify full citation metadata before manuscript insertion.

## 1. Closest direct conversion-microkinetic precedent

Alsaç et al., ACS Applied Materials & Interfaces (2026)  
DOI: **10.1021/acsami.5c20956**

Why relevant:
- explicit microkinetic modeling of conversion cathodes including S, FeS2, and FeF3;
- reaction networks + Butler–Volmer kinetics;
- species evolution, transport/passivation;
- distinguishes initial and subsequent reaction pathways.

Difference from current HEO direction:
- their emphasis is species/voltage/pressure evolution;
- the present HEO idea specifically links multi-step conversion microkinetics to **current-interruption relaxation amplitude, timescale, and cycle evolution**.

## 2. FeF3 GITT / conversion overpotential

JACS (2016)  
DOI: **10.1021/jacs.6b00061**

Why relevant:
- GITT and conversion overpotential;
- discusses contributions from new-phase nucleation/growth, mass transport, spatial heterogeneity, and nanophase interfacial penalties;
- useful precedent that conversion overpotential/relaxation cannot be reduced automatically to one charge-transfer resistance.

## 3. NiO nucleation / conversion

ACS Nano (2019)  
DOI: **10.1021/acsnano.9b02007**

Why relevant:
- potential-dependent nucleation barrier;
- theoretical conversion potential vs much lower potential needed for bulk conversion;
- supports an activation/nucleation/growth decomposition.

## 4. NiO heterogeneous nucleation / phase-front evolution

Nature Communications article: **ncomms4358**

Why relevant:
- first-cycle vs subsequent-cycle reaction behavior differs;
- heterogeneous nucleation and evolving phase fronts;
- useful for interpreting why first-cycle conversion-associated relaxation need not be stationary.

## 5. FeS2 continuum / P2D conversion modeling

Journal of Power Sources (2022)  
PII: **S0378775322008783**

Why relevant:
- conversion with changing active area, product shell resistance, transport, and rate limitation;
- shows why a single scalar kinetic constant is generally insufficient for conversion electrodes.

## 6. Literature gap to keep investigating

Potentially underdeveloped combination:

**elementary-step/RDS-style electrochemical microkinetics**
+
**current-interruption/open-circuit internal-state relaxation**
+
**cycle-dependent amplitude/timescale evolution**

Do not make a “first” claim yet.

## 7. Follow-up paper direction

If expanded experimentally:
- pulse-current dependence at matched reaction state;
- oxide vs sulfide conversion comparison;
- use current-on polarization + current-off relaxation jointly to narrow mechanism space.

Potential broad question:

**Can current-on and current-off electrochemical responses constrain the rate-limiting steps of conversion reactions?**
